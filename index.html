Meet the voice behind the letter

Hi friend, I'm Rebekah.

I'm a homeschooling mother of four, a scratch cook, and an allergy mom. After eleven years of navigating food allergies in our family, I know that mealtimes can hold both hard questions and moments of real joy.

I created Beyond the Ingredients to share encouragement, practical ideas, and the kind of recipes that bring everyone back to the table.

I'm also writing Everyday Safe Plates, my upcoming children's food allergy cookbook. Newsletter readers will hear about its journey toward Amazon first.

“This isn't only about what we leave out of a meal. It's about the joy, flavor, and peace we can bring back.”
